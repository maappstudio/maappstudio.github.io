Arrowtune

Arrow puzzle game for Android

Tap an arrow. If its way is clear, it flies off the board - and plays a note. Clear every arrow and you have played a tune. Arrowtune is a calm, clever puzzle about order: which arrow can go first?

How to play

  1. Tap an arrow whose path to the edge is clear - it flies away.
  2. A blocked arrow turns red and costs a heart.
  3. Clear every arrow to win. No mistakes = three stars.
