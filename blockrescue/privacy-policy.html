Block Rescue

Privacy policy · MA App Studio

In short

Block Rescue is made by MA App Studio ("we"). It is a puzzle game you play on your own phone. It has no account and never asks who you are. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information for those ads, as explained below.

What the game keeps

Your progress - levels won and their stars, coins, boosters, the animals you saved, your island decorations, daily gifts and Daily Rescue streak, your Classic best score - and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminder). This is saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: videos you can choose to watch for a reward (a revive, continuing a Classic game, double coins, a double daily gift, a free booster, free coins or saving your daily streak), an ad between levels now and then, and a banner at the bottom of menu screens. To show and count these ads, Google may collect your phone's advertising ID, your rough location from your internet address, your phone type and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as its own policy says: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . Where the law requires it (for example in Europe and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads).

Game statistics

To make levels fairer, the game counts things like which level you play and whether you win. In this version these counts stay on your phone and are not sent anywhere. If a future version sends anonymous statistics (for example with Google Firebase), this policy will say so first.

What the game does not do

No account or sign-in. No names, email addresses, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. No purchases in this version. The game itself does not collect or sell anything about you; the only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob uses for ads, described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to store or delete. Your progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (phone Settings > Apps > Block Rescue > Storage), or with Settings > Reset progress in the game. Data Google collects for ads is kept and deleted as Google's policy says, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

Security

Everything the ads library sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). Your progress never leaves your phone.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game shows one reminder a day when your daily gift or Daily Rescue is waiting. It is set up on your phone; no server is involved. You can turn it off in Settings > Daily reminder, or in your phone's settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's own share menu opens with a short text about the game. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Children

Block Rescue is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that concerns you, write to us.

Contact

MA App Studio. Questions about this policy or about your data: write to maappstudio.dev@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is used, this policy will be updated first and the date below will change.
