How to play

Drag a block onto the board.

Fill a whole row or column to clear it.

Clear the lines through a cage to free the animal - before the water reaches it!

Questions

Is Block Rescue free?

Yes. The game is free to play and shows ads. There is nothing to buy.

Do I need the internet?

No. You can play every level offline. Ads only show when you are online.

Does it collect my data?

The game keeps your progress only on your phone. Google AdMob uses some device data for ads - see the privacy policy.

Will there be more levels?

Yes! New worlds with new levels come with updates.

Who is it for?

Block Rescue is made for players aged 13 and over.
