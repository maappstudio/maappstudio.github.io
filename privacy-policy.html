Arrowtune

Privacy policy · MA App Studio

In short

Arrowtune is made by MA App Studio ("we"). It is a puzzle game you play on your own phone. It does not ask who you are and has no account. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information for those ads, as explained below.

What the game keeps

Your progress - levels played and their stars and times, daily puzzles solved, badges, hints left, the level you left half way - and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminder, theme, arrow style and the "play your way" options). This is saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a video you can choose to watch for a free hint or an extra heart, an ad between levels now and then, and a banner on the Levels screen. To show and count these ads, Google may collect your phone's advertising ID, your rough location from your internet address, your phone type and ad interactions. Google uses this as its own policy says: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . Where the law requires it (for example in Europe and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your answer in Me > Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads).

What the game does not do

No account or sign-in. No names, email addresses, contacts, photos or microphone. The game itself does not collect or sell anything about you; the only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob uses for ads, described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to store or delete. Your progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (phone Settings > Apps > Arrowtune > Storage). Data Google collects for ads is kept and deleted as Google's policy says, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

Security

Everything the ads library sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). Your progress never leaves your phone.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a daily puzzle, your phone's own share menu opens with a short text (the date, your stars, time and streak). Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game shows one reminder a day when the daily puzzle is waiting. It is set up on your phone; no server is involved. You can turn it off in Me > Settings > Daily reminder, or in your phone's settings.

Vibration

The game uses your phone's vibration for a wrong move. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

Arrowtune is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

MA App Studio. Questions about this policy or about your data: write to maappstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is used, this policy will be updated first and the date below will change.
